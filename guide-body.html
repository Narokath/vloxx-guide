Group assignments

The targeting plan: fixate always gets a green. Melee stay closest to Vloxx so the other two greens usually select melee players; ranged players and healers stay farther back. Then put one green at melee, one at ranged, and one with healers/fixate.

Build three green groups around fixate, melee and ranged. Each green needs at least three valid players, including the marked player. With ten players, there is one extra body to balance the groups. A player standing in two greens counts for neither.

“Melee” and “ranged” are formation assignments. They give everyone a predictable place to go when the markers appear; use the phase’s diagram for the actual positions.

Fixate, Ascension and boon strip

Fixate: follow the diamond

The purple diamond aims Vloxx’s directed attacks. A normal assignment lasts 60 seconds. It can end early, especially when the fixated player goes down or dies. Weapon Aspects choose their own targets.

After natural expiry, the next assignment is typically about eight seconds later. It can be almost immediate or delayed for much longer by encounter timing. There is no dependable fixed swap countdown, and the same player can be chosen again. Keep watching for the new diamond and call it immediately.

Ascension and Empowered: two linked resources

  • Ascension belongs to each player. Start with ten stacks. Losing stacks makes ordinary boon stripping stronger; reaching zero is fatal.
  • Empowered belongs to Vloxx. Each Ascension stack lost adds one stack of his Empowered damage buff. A death can add the player’s remaining stacks, so one mistake can increase pressure on the whole squad. Breaking his intermission defiance bar also grants him 10 Empowered stacks.
  • Orbs reverse the exchange. Collect an Ascension orb to recover one missing stack, up to ten, and remove one Empowered stack from Vloxx. Prioritise players missing Ascension when it is safe to collect.

Weapon breakbars produce three orbs when Vloxx has Empowered stacks. At zero Empowered, no new weapon orbs were recorded. Orbs already on the floor are separate; don’t wait for another weapon bar to produce more at zero stacks.

How ordinary boon removal works

Boon types removed = the smaller of available boon types and 11 − current Ascension. Removing Might means the whole boon type, potentially all 25 stacks. It does not mean removing one Might stack.

Ordinary boon types removed12345678910

For example, a failed large green takes a player from ten to seven Ascension. Subsequent ordinary stripping can remove four boon types per eligible hit, rather than one. Recovering Ascension reduces this pressure again.

Ordinary removal strongly favours the most recently applied or refreshed boon types. A fresh cover boon can be selected ahead of Quickness or Alacrity, but missing stacks allow more types to be removed. Concurrent applications and overlapping attacks can defeat that plan.

Keep the exceptions separate: Teleport corrupts many boon types at once; Aegis used to block an attack and Stability consumed by crowd control are not ordinary arbitrary-boon selection. Some failed Greens also remove Stability in addition to their ordinary quota.

Ascension loss attribution

The cause table counts surviving-player loss episodes, excluding down/death within 100 ms, and matching the same victim and source to a known failure/corruption packet within 100 ms. Large-green losses are three stacks; Shackles and Teleport losses are one. Every surviving-player loss episode in this check maps to one of those three families. Strip attribution requires one matching positive direct-damage skill from the same attacker within 5 ms, with a separate short window for Teleport. Close removal packets are grouped within 5 ms. Down/death-adjacent episodes are excluded. Internal-marker timing uses the recording character because those effects are not consistently logged for the rest of the squad. Damage components, overlaps, boon availability and Stability consumption limit what can be inferred about a mechanic’s exact ICD.

How these timings were checked

All 509 indexed recordings were considered: 426 CM and 45 LCM recordings enter these comparisons; 38 normal-mode recordings are excluded. For Greens and Puddles, warning effects are identified by their content GUID in each log, with Vloxx as source. Marks within one second are grouped into a round, timed at its first recorded mark.

P4 starts at Vloxx’s Final Form channel, not the end of the preceding breakbar. The P4 Greens check contains 216 CM rounds from 92 recordings and 24 LCM rounds from eight recordings, giving 124 CM and 16 LCM repeat intervals. Puddles contains 218 CM rounds and 24 LCM rounds, giving 126 CM and 16 LCM intervals. Typical first marks are +3 seconds for Greens and +23 seconds for Puddles, measured from the channel start.

The P4 Greens median is 30.002 seconds in both modes; a first recorded mark can shift by about 0.08 seconds between targets, so the shortest measured gaps are just below 30 seconds. Some CM gaps are about 60 seconds because no intervening marker round was recorded. Puddles has a 20.000-second median in both modes. These observations do not prove the reason for a missing round.

For other mechanics, only Vloxx’s animation starts count. Each pair has the same actor, skill and health band, with 1–180 seconds between starts. Health bands label P1–P3 but do not identify every split boundary exactly. Interruptions and the rest of the rotation can affect gaps. A recording can contribute more than one interval.

P1–P3 CD window: ≥40.0 s after cast ends · observedP1–P3 CD window: ≥40.0 s after cast ends · observedP4 warnings: about every 30 s

All phases. In P4, warnings typically begin around +3 seconds from the Final Form channel start and repeat about every 30 seconds.

Large greens require at least three valid soakers per circle, including the marked player. Stand in one green only; overlaps count for neither.

Before selection: fixate always gets one green. Melee stand closest to Vloxx to bait the other two; ranged players and healers stand farther back. The aim is one green for each of the melee, ranged and healer/fixate groups.

  1. Two melee players get greens: one green stays with melee and one goes to the ranged position. Both initially move toward ranged; the player ahead continues, while the player behind stops and takes the melee position.
  2. A ranged player gets a green: they take the ranged position. The melee player with the other green stays melee; no exchange is needed.
  3. A healer is fixated: both healers together are only two players, so the floater joins them. Otherwise the floater’s default is melee.
  4. The floater is fixated: they join both healers. If a melee player takes a green to ranged and leaves only two at melee, send a ranged player back to make three.
  5. An unexpected selection or swap changes the numbers: call it and fill whichever green needs a third player. The goal is three valid groups, not moving greens for its own sake.

A failed green removes Ascension and strengthens Vloxx. Stand inside one circle only; overlapping greens do not count as a valid soak.

Floater: join fixate’s green if a healer is fixated; otherwise join the melee group. Keep both healers from baiting separate greens.

P4: Bunker is CM-only. Boneskinner keeps the groups separated and is viable in LCM; preserve the formation while moving.

Ascension

Ascension · personal stacks

Stacking encounter effect

Start with ten personal stacks. Failed Greens cost three; failed P1 Shackles and P3 Teleport corruption cost one. Zero Ascension is fatal.

Missing stacks increase ordinary boon removal: at ten Ascension an eligible hit removes one boon type; at seven it can remove four.

Collect an orb to recover one missing stack, up to ten. Prioritise players missing Ascension when it is safe to collect.

Empowered

Empowered · Vloxx’s stacks

Stacking encounter effect

Lost Ascension adds to Vloxx’s Empowered damage buff. Each stack lost adds one Empowered; death can transfer a player’s remaining stacks.

Collecting an Ascension orb removes one Empowered stack. Keep weapons under control and collect accessible orbs between mechanics.

Advanced · early Staff bait in recent pulls
